Real · concept redesign

Small UX changes that make Real faster to watch with.

An unsolicited redesign of three core screens. Same design system, type, and brand. No rebrand. Each change fixes one specific point of friction while watching a live game. Every "after" below is a working mockup, not a flat image.

Scope3 screens
ApproachSame design system, sharper UX
Built withHand-coded HTML / CSS
01 · Scores home

Finding your sport and event, faster

Before
After
The problem

The scores you came for sit behind two long rows of text tabs. Switching sports means scrolling plain labels: slow to scan, easy to overshoot. And the World Cup is buried mid-scroll.

  • 1
    Sport selector → icon pillsA recognizable emoji per sport. Scan by shape, not by reading. Shorter than the old text row.
  • 2
    World Cup featured calloutThe event people open this screen for, surfaced as a live card. Other leagues stay one tap away.
  • 3
    Players / Countries filterOne toggle switches the ranking between players and national teams. No new screen.
  • 4
    Cleaner match cardsFlag, score, then country name in grey. Live and Final badges sit top-right so names never wrap.
02 · Play detail

The player's night, without leaving the play

Before
After
The problem

The play is here, but the player's stats for the game are not. A big Performance button dominates the screen and still sends you elsewhere for points, rebounds, assists.

  • 1
    Game-stat card under the playPTS, REB, AST up top. FG, 3PT, BLK, STL, MIN below. The box score in one glance.
  • 2
    Smaller Performance buttonStill here for the deep dive, now a compact action inside the card, not a banner.
  • 3
    Tighter hierarchyThe play title leads. Attribution lines shrink. Eye goes play, stats, chart.
03 · Comments

Chat that still feels like the game

Before
After
The problem

Opening comments feels like leaving the game. Chat takes the full screen, and the exit is a close button stranded bottom-right, where no one looks for back.

  • 1
    Back moved to top-leftThe expected spot for exit. The scorebug shrinks into a compact game bar.
  • 2
    Chat as an in-game sheetA panel with a grab handle sits over the game, not in place of it.
  • 3
    Chat / Plays / Stats tabsComments become one tab of the live game, so you never feel moved away.
  • 4
    Send affordance fixedThe composer's ✕ becomes an ↑ send arrow.
